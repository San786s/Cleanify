About Cleanify

Bangalore's Most Trusted Cleaning Service Since 2019

Our Story

Founded in 2019, Cleanify started with a simple mission: to provide Bangalore with professional, reliable, and eco-friendly cleaning services. What began as a small team of three cleaning enthusiasts has grown into Bangalore's most trusted cleaning company with over 50 trained professionals serving hundreds of satisfied customers.

Our Mission

To transform living and working spaces into clean, healthy environments using sustainable practices and cutting-edge technology.

Our Vision

To become Bangalore's leading cleaning service provider known for reliability, quality, and environmental responsibility.

Our Values

Quality, Integrity, Sustainability, Customer Satisfaction, and Innovation drive everything we do.
